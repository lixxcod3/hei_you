About Hei You

The project, the background, and the six students building it — one brick at a time.

1 What is the Project?

The project is the development of Hei You (嗨，你), a web-based educational platform designed to teach C programming fundamentals to beginners. The system features interactive tutorials, syntax highlighting, and an in-browser compiler simulation. The learning experience is guided by the persona of Professor Wáng, who helps students build their knowledge “one brick at a time.” The platform provides dynamic, personality-driven feedback based on student performance.

2 Origin & Background

This project addresses the core fundamentals of the low-level programming language C. Beginners are often intimidated by cryptic compiler errors, memory-management complexities, and the lack of engaging interfaces. By providing a guided, web-based experience, the project breaks down complex syntax into digestible modules. The inclusion of Professor Wáng — who offers blunt but warm reactions — creates a highly engaging and memorable feedback loop that keeps users motivated:

  • “Proud of you.” — on success
  • “Shame on you.” — on a syntax or logic error
  • “See you.” — when a chapter or module is completed
