About Us

Building Hei You

Meet the six students building a better way to learn C — one brick at a time.

1 What is Hei You?

Learning C usually means staring at cryptic terminal errors and pulling your hair out over memory leaks. We thought, why not make it fun?

Hei You (嗨，你) is a web-based playground that breaks C down into bite-sized, interactive chunks. No downloads, no messy setups—just a clean browser compiler and a little tough love from our virtual mentor, Professor Wáng.

2 The Professor Wáng Experience

Instead of standard red error text, you get real feedback from Professor Wáng. He’s blunt, he’s warm, and he definitely has opinions about your code. Try it out:

"Well? Write some code. Let's see what you've got."
