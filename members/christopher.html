the habit

Keeping watch on what's arriving

From a very young age I've kept up with where technology is going. It has never once felt like homework. The field moves fast and I want to see everything it has to offer.

the reputation

Friends call me the creative one

Friends and classmates describe me as a highly creative person. I like working with my imagination, taking a simple idea and building it out until it's something unique and completely my own.

Creativity isn't only about thinking differently. It's also about having the practical skills to turn a bright idea into something real.

the reason for CS

Getting the childhood ideas out

My goal is to bring the wild, wonderful imaginations I had as a kid into the real world. That might be a fun interactive video game, or a whole new kind of digital experience. Things people can genuinely enjoy.
